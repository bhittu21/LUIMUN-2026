## 🏛️ About the Conference

In **2016**, a passionate group of students at **Leading University** took a historic first step: founding the **Leading University Model United Nations Association (LUMUNA)** and hosting Sylhet’s first-ever Model United Nations conference. 

A decade later, **LUIMUN returns for its landmark 5th International Edition**, celebrating **10 years** of regional and international diplomatic excellence. For this anniversary edition, the conference expands across **four full days**, enabling deeper multilateral negotiations, crisis simulations, and high-impact policy debate than ever before.

## 🇺🇳 Committees & Chambers

LUIMUN 2026 features **10 dynamic committee simulations**, accommodating novice delegates, seasoned debaters, and international affairs scholars:

1. **UNSC** — United Nations Security Council
2. **UNHRC** — United Nations Human Rights Council
3. **UNEP** — United Nations Environment Programme
4. **UNDP** — United Nations Development Programme
5. **UNODC** — United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime
6. **UN Women** — United Nations Entity for Gender Equality & the Empowerment of Women
7. **SPECPOL** — Special Political and Decolonization Committee
8. **IP** — International Press Corps
9. **Surprise Committee 01** — Specialized High-Stakes Crisis Simulation
10. **Surprise Committee 02** — Fast-Paced Multilateral Cabinet
